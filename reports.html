Reports

From building data to decision clarity.

MEIFERTS reports translate technical building information into structured, decision-oriented insight for buyers, investors and real estate stakeholders.

Report 01

Technical Property Review™

A structured technical review of the building, its condition, maintenance profile and visible or documented risk factors.

The objective is to understand the asset beyond listing information, surface impressions and sales material.

Report 02

Building Risk Score™

A standardized risk classification designed to translate technical observations into an understandable risk framework.

The score does not replace professional judgement. It supports it.

Report 03

Lifecycle Assessment™

Buildings age in phases. A lifecycle assessment helps identify whether an asset is stabilized, mature, aging or entering a capital-intensive period.

Report 04

CAPEX Forecast™

Future capital expenditure requirements are translated into a planning framework across short-, medium- and long-term periods.

Report 05

Decision Support Report™

The final synthesis connects building condition, lifecycle risk, governance quality and capital expenditure exposure.

It answers the questions that matter before a property decision becomes expensive.

Core Questions

  • Is the building technically sound?
  • What are the key risks?
  • What could it cost later?
  • What remains uncertain?
  • Would the asset still make sense after technical review?
