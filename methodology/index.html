THE MEIFERTS STANDARD™

Better decisions begin with better understanding.

Most property decisions are made based on visible factors such as location, design and purchase price. Many of the most significant risks remain hidden. The MEIFERTS Standard™ was developed to create technical clarity before important decisions are made.

Observe.

Every building tells a story. The first step is observation. We review the asset, its condition, maintenance history, visible deficiencies, technical systems and available documentation. The objective is not to collect information. The objective is to understand what the building is trying to tell us.

Understand.

Buildings are complex systems where individual observations rarely exist in isolation. Water intrusion may indicate building envelope deficiencies. Repeated repairs may indicate larger maintenance concerns. This phase focuses on understanding relationships and implications rather than isolated findings.

Evaluate.

Technical findings are evaluated through the lens of condition, risk, lifecycle implications and future capital expenditure requirements. Not every defect matters. Some do. Understanding the difference is critical.

Decide.

The final objective is not a report. The final objective is a better decision. Every assessment should help answer practical questions regarding technical condition, future expenditure and ownership risk.
