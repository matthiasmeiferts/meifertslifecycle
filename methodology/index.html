The MEIFERTS Standard™

From Observation to Decision Clarity.

Every building generates observations. The MEIFERTS Standard™ transforms them into structured understanding, consistent evaluation and better property decisions.

Buildings are complex systems. Their risks are rarely contained in a single defect, a single drawing or a single viewing. They emerge through relationships between condition, maintenance, lifecycle, governance and future capital expenditure.

Why Methodology Matters

Information alone does not create better decisions.

A technical inspection can generate hundreds of observations: cracks, stains, corrosion, ageing systems, missing documents, weak reserves, deferred maintenance and incomplete repairs.

On their own, these findings are only information. They become valuable when they are interpreted, connected and evaluated in relation to the decision that must be made.

The MEIFERTS Standard™ was developed to structure this process. It is not designed to replace professional judgement. It is designed to make that judgement transparent, consistent and decision-oriented.

Observe

Evidence before opinion.

Every meaningful decision begins with observation. Not assumptions. Not sales language. Evidence.

Buildings communicate through construction details, material ageing, moisture behaviour, technical systems, maintenance quality and operational history.

Observation is not yet analysis. It is the foundation upon which all later understanding depends.

Understand

Context creates knowledge.

A crack may indicate harmless shrinkage or structural movement. Moisture may result from condensation, a leaking pipe or a failing building envelope.

The significance of a finding only becomes apparent when interpreted within the broader context of the building.

Without context there is only information. With context there is knowledge.

Evaluate

Judgement creates priority.

Every observation and every conclusion must answer a practical question: how does this influence the decision?

The evaluation process considers technical risk, lifecycle risk, financial risk and governance risk together rather than individually.

Evaluation requires judgement rather than simple defect counting.

Decide

Clarity supports action.

The final purpose of the MEIFERTS Standard™ is not inspection. It is decision support.

The appropriate decision may be to proceed, renegotiate, adjust investment planning, request specialist investigation, strengthen contractual protection or walk away.

Building Intelligence reduces uncertainty to a level where informed decisions become possible.

Components of the Standard

A complete decision process.

The MEIFERTS Standard™ is not a single report. It is a structured framework consisting of complementary components. Each component answers a different question.

Technical Property Review™

Systematic observation of physical condition, visible deficiencies, maintenance quality and building performance.

Building Risk Score™

Structured risk classification based on technical evidence, ownership relevance and future implications.

Lifecycle Assessment™

Evaluation of ageing behaviour, remaining useful life and long-term ownership implications.

CAPEX Forecast™

Foreseeable capital expenditure based on lifecycle behaviour, technical observations and maintenance history.

Decision Support Report™

Structured recommendation designed for decision makers rather than engineers.
