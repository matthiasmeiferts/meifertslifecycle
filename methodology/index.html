The MEIFERTS Standard™

From Observation to Decision Clarity.

Building Intelligence is the structured process of transforming technical building observations into reliable decision support through systematic analysis, lifecycle assessment and risk evaluation.

Buildings generate information. Cracks. Moisture. Ageing. Maintenance. Documentation. Financial reserves. On their own, these observations remain individual facts. Facts do not create better decisions. Understanding does.

Why a Standard?

Information alone does not create understanding.

Every building is different. Construction methods vary. Materials age differently. Maintenance quality changes over decades. Ownership structures influence investment decisions. Climate affects deterioration.

Yet despite these differences, every professional assessment should follow the same logical process. Not because every building is identical, but because every meaningful decision requires the same sequence of thinking.

The MEIFERTS Standard™ provides a consistent framework that allows complex technical information to become understandable, comparable and actionable. It is not designed to replace professional judgement. It is designed to structure it.

Observe

Evidence before opinion.

Every meaningful decision begins with observation. Not assumptions. Not sales language. Evidence.

Buildings continuously communicate their condition through visible and measurable indicators: construction details, material ageing, moisture behaviour, technical systems, maintenance quality and operational history.

Without reliable observations, every later conclusion becomes uncertain. Observation is not yet analysis. It is the foundation upon which all later understanding depends.

Understand

Context creates knowledge.

A crack may indicate harmless shrinkage or structural movement. Moisture may result from condensation, a leaking pipe or a failing building envelope.

Their significance only becomes apparent when interpreted within the broader context of the building. Understanding means recognising relationships rather than isolated defects.

Without context there is only information. With context there is knowledge.

Evaluate

Judgement creates priority.

Understanding alone is not sufficient. Every observation and every conclusion must answer a practical question: how does this influence the decision?

The evaluation process considers technical risk, lifecycle risk, financial risk and governance risk together rather than individually.

A technically minor defect may create significant financial consequences. Evaluation therefore requires judgement rather than simple defect counting.

Decide

Clarity supports action.

The final purpose of the MEIFERTS Standard™ is not inspection. It is decision support.

The appropriate decision may be to proceed, renegotiate, adjust investment planning, request specialist investigation, strengthen contractual protection or walk away.

Building Intelligence reduces uncertainty to a level where informed, transparent and confident decisions become possible.

Components of the Standard

A complete decision process.

The MEIFERTS Standard™ is not a single report. It is a structured framework consisting of complementary components. Each component answers a different question. Together they create a complete decision process.

Technical Property Review™

Systematic observation of physical condition, visible deficiencies, maintenance quality and building performance.

Building Risk Score™

Structured risk classification based on technical evidence, ownership relevance and future implications.

Lifecycle Assessment™

Evaluation of ageing behaviour, remaining useful life and long-term ownership implications.

CAPEX Forecast™

Foreseeable capital expenditure based on lifecycle behaviour, technical observations and maintenance history.

Decision Support Report™

Structured recommendation designed for decision makers rather than engineers.

Purpose

More than documentation.

Buildings rarely fail because of a single defect. More often, problems emerge when small observations remain disconnected and their long-term implications are overlooked.

The purpose of the MEIFERTS Standard™ is therefore not simply to identify technical issues.

Its purpose is to connect evidence, explain relationships and transform complexity into practical understanding.

Closing Statement

Better understanding creates better decisions.

The MEIFERTS Standard™ connects observation, understanding, evaluation and decision support into one structured process.

It exists because property decisions are rarely improved by more information alone. They improve when information is interpreted, risks are structured and decisions become clear.
