The MEIFERTS Standard™

Observe. Understand. Evaluate. Decide.

The platform is built around one principle: better decisions begin before the purchase, before the negotiation and before capital expenditure becomes unavoidable.

Buildings generate evidence through age, condition, documentation, maintenance culture and visible deterioration. MEIFERTS turns this evidence into understanding.

Why buildings matter

Risk rarely appears overnight.

Most technical risk develops quietly. Maintenance is deferred, systems age, documentation becomes incomplete and future capital expenditure accumulates.

Technical due diligence recognises this process before a decision is made.

01

Observe

Collect visible and documented evidence from the building, its systems, context and maintenance history.

02

Understand

Interpret conditions within the building lifecycle, technical context and ownership reality.

03

Evaluate

Translate findings into risk categories, capital exposure and decision-relevant priorities.

04

Decide

Support purchase, negotiation, further investigation or decline with structured technical clarity.
