Request Review

Request a technical property review.

Before price, yield or lifestyle comes the building itself. A technical review helps identify risks, lifecycle issues and future CAPEX exposure before decisions become expensive.

What to Submit

Please provide the basic property information available to you. A first review does not require perfect documentation. It begins with what is already known.

  • Property type and location
  • Purpose of review
  • Available documents or listing information
  • Known concerns or visible defects
  • Decision timeline

Review Focus

Depending on the asset and available information, the review may focus on building condition, maintenance risks, lifecycle exposure, governance quality and possible future capital expenditure.

The objective is not to create certainty. The objective is to improve understanding before a decision is made.
