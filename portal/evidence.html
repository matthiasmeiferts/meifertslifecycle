Evidence Principle

No finding without evidence.

Every observation, finding, assessment, recommendation and report statement must be traceable to one or more evidence objects.

Evidence can be a photo, document, note, measurement, thermal image or client-provided record. The platform treats it as structured decision material, not as loose file storage.
