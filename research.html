MEIFERTS Research

Building knowledge for better property decisions.

Research, methodology papers and technical notes on building risk, lifecycle behaviour, deferred maintenance, CAPEX exposure and Technical Due Diligence.

MEIFERTS Building Intelligence treats research as part of the decision process. The objective is not academic complexity. The objective is to translate building complexity into usable, structured and decision-relevant understanding.

Research Focus

Where building performance becomes decision risk.

Buildings change over time. Materials age, systems deteriorate, maintenance decisions compound, reserves become relevant and hidden defects slowly become financial exposure.

The research section documents the frameworks behind the MEIFERTS Standard™: how buildings are observed, how evidence is structured, how lifecycle behaviour is interpreted and how findings become decision support.

Research Principles

Research must improve decisions, not decorate documents.

The purpose of MEIFERTS research is practical: to create frameworks that help buyers, investors and advisors understand what a building may ask of them after acquisition.

Evidence before opinion

Every research model begins with observable condition, documentation and context.

Lifecycle before appearance

Buildings should be understood through long-term performance, not only present visual condition.

Risk before price

The acquisition price is only one part of the ownership decision.

Decision before documentation

Reports are only useful when they support a clear next step.
