Case Study 01

Condominium Review
Pattaya, Thailand

Illustrative example demonstrating how technical findings can be translated into decision-oriented building intelligence.

Asset Overview

Property Summary

Asset Type: Condominium

Location: Pattaya, Thailand

Building Age: Mature Asset

Review Focus: Technical Condition, Maintenance Profile, Governance Indicators and Future CAPEX Exposure.

Building Risk Score™
72

Classification: Moderate Risk Profile

No critical structural concerns identified. Several areas require further investigation prior to acquisition.

Key Findings

  • Early waterproofing observations in selected areas.
  • Maintenance records partially available.
  • Reserve fund transparency limited.
  • Deferred maintenance indicators present.
  • No major immediate life-safety concerns observed.

Lifecycle Assessment™

The asset appears to be in a mature lifecycle phase.

Most building systems remain functional but future replacement planning should be expected over the coming years.

Future capital expenditure requirements are likely to increase gradually rather than suddenly.

CAPEX Considerations

  • Waterproofing reserves
  • Mechanical equipment replacement planning
  • Common area refurbishment
  • Building envelope maintenance
  • Long-term reserve adequacy review

Decision Summary

Technically acceptable.

No immediate deal-breaking conditions identified.

Additional document review and targeted technical verification recommended before acquisition.

MEIFERTS Perspective

The purpose of a technical review is not to predict the future with certainty. It is to understand which risks are visible, which remain uncertain and which decisions deserve closer attention.
