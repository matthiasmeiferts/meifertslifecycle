Methodology

Why the method can be trusted.

The MEIFERTS methodology does not begin with opinion. It begins with evidence.

Buildings generate evidence through age, condition, documentation, maintenance culture and visible deterioration. The methodology translates this evidence into understanding, risk and decision confidence.

Evidence

Observations, documents, measurements and maintenance records create the factual basis.

Understanding

Technical findings are interpreted within lifecycle, climate, use and governance context.

Confidence

Priorities, risk categories and capital horizons reduce uncertainty.

Decision

The report turns technical complexity into a usable property decision.

Technical Property Review™

A structured review of the building as a technical system.

The review identifies technical conditions that may influence risk, negotiations, ownership strategy and future capital expenditure.

  • Building envelope, roof and façade
  • Moisture pathways and visible defects
  • MEP systems and common areas
  • Documentation and maintenance culture
Deliverable

From findings to usable decisions.

The output connects observed conditions with risk, lifecycle stage, CAPEX implications and recommendations.

Building Risk Score™

Findings alone are not enough.

Technical observations become useful only when they are prioritised. The Building Risk Score™ translates visible and documented evidence into risk levels that support negotiation, further investigation or purchase decisions.

60–79Moderate risk

Lifecycle Assessment™

Technical ageing becomes investment timing.

Remaining useful life, deferred maintenance, replacement cycles and the interaction between building systems determine whether an asset is stable, mature or capital intensive.

  • Age and condition of major systems
  • Deferred or reactive maintenance
  • Interaction between envelope, moisture and MEP systems
  • Likely intervention windows before ownership decisions
