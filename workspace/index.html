Platform Logic

From checklist to decision clarity.

The workspace is not a standalone checklist. It is the field interface of the MEIFERTS Building Intelligence system.

Inspection inputs are captured as structured data and can be translated into risk scoring, lifecycle assessment, CAPEX forecasting and report output.

Gutachter Workflow

One asset. One structured review.

Each project begins with an asset, continues through technical observation and ends with decision-oriented reporting.

The goal is not to collect more information. The goal is to create understanding.
